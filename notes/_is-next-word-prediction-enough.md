# Is predicting the next word enough?

*Opening: language models are trained to predict the next token. The question is whether that can ever amount to understanding the world.*

## LeCun's argument

*His main points, one short paragraph each: errors compounding in autoregressive generation, how little of human knowledge is in text, and the absence of planning.*

## The counter-arguments

*The strongest replies: what language models demonstrably do, and the view that prediction at scale forces a world model.*

## The alternative on offer

*[JEPA](jepa.html) (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) as LeCun's proposed alternative: learn from sensory data, predict in an abstract space.*
