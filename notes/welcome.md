This page is a small personal repository of things I'm reading and writing. Notes
like this are written in **Markdown** (just a text file); documents I'm reading sit
here as **PDFs**.

## How Markdown works

A few basics — that's really all you need:

- `# Heading` makes a big heading, `## Heading` a smaller one.
- `**bold**` gives **bold**, `*italic*` gives *italic*.
- Start a line with `- ` to make a bullet.
- `[link text](https://example.com)` makes a [link](https://example.com).
