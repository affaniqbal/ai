# What should I read on world models?

*Opening: who this list is for and how it is ordered.*

Much of it centres on [JEPA](jepa.html) (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture), Yann LeCun's proposal for learning by predicting abstract representations.

## Start here

- [A Path Towards Autonomous Machine Intelligence](https://openreview.net/forum?id=BZ5a1r-kVsf) (LeCun, 2022). *One or two lines on why.*

- [I-JEPA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.08243) (2023). *One or two lines.*
- [V-JEPA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08471) (2024). *One or two lines.*
- [V-JEPA 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09985) (2025). *One or two lines.*
- [LeJEPA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08544) (2025). *One or two lines.*
