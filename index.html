About

I'm Affan — a physicist with a PhD from Cambridge, where I applied computer-vision techniques to energy materials.

I've been working in machine learning since 2017, starting with my bachelor's thesis. My work since has ranged widely: from molecular modelling and Gaussian processes to more applied problems like building AI agents and verifiers, along with commercial training at ERM.

What holds my attention now is AI beyond large language models — systems that build genuine, structured models of the world.

This site is where I collect notes on the ideas I'm exploring.
